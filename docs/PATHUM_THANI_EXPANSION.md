## Mosque coverage

CICOT currently lists many Pathum Thani mosques. Batch 1 samples Mueang Pathum
Thani, Khlong Luang and Lam Luk Ka, including Al Ala, Jamiul Islam, Ruam Chit
Muslim, Darul Ibadah and Kaeo Nimit.

This is not a complete provincial mosque inventory.

## Restaurant evidence

Current Wongnai data supports Subaidah Chuan Chim Rangsit, Hi Lan and Habebee
Halal Food as active Muslim/Halal venues.

These remain `MUSLIM_FRIENDLY` unless explicit Muslim ownership or a current
official halal certificate is attached.

## Accommodation gap

No Pathum Thani accommodation is included in Batch 1 because current search
results did not provide sufficiently strong halal dining/breakfast evidence for
a conservative production seed.

This is a data-quality hold, not a claim that Muslim-friendly accommodation is
absent from Pathum Thani.
