## Mosque coverage

The province has more than five CICOT-listed mosques. Batch 1 prioritizes three
Cha-am/Sam Phraya mosques plus the provincial central mosque area at Ban Laem
and one Mueang Phetchaburi mosque.

This is a tourism-oriented first batch, not a complete mosque inventory.
