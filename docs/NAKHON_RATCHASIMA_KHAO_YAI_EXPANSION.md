## Why the mosque count is two

The current CICOT page surfaced Jannatul Firdaus in Pak Chong and Ar-Ridwan
(Nana) in Mueang Nakhon Ratchasima. Batch 1 uses only records directly supported
by the current source rather than forcing a fixed mosque quota.

Jannatul Firdaus is particularly relevant to the Khao Yai route because it is in
Pak Chong.

## Trust policy

Commercial candidates remain `MUSLIM_FRIENDLY`.

A Muslim/Halal category, halal menu, halal breakfast option or guest/property
statement does not itself establish Muslim ownership or current official
certification. Do not promote to `MUSLIM_OWNED` or `HALAL_CERTIFIED` without
evidence that directly supports that stronger claim.
